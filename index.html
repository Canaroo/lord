WHERE DO YOUR THUMBS GO?

Drag the glowing line up or down to where your thumbs feel comfortable. Notes are hit on the line, and you can tap anywhere in a lane below it.

SET UP TIMING?

Phones and wireless earbuds add their own sound delay. A 10-second tap test lines the notes up with what you hear. You can redo it any time in Options.
